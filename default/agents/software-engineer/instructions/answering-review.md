Code Review's stage on open threads holds the verdict words and the grounds to accept, decline or defer. The register of a reply:

- Comply with the commit and the test that pins it: "Fixed in <hash>. Covered by 'rejects a duplicate email even when another row is still blank'". A bare "fixed" is not enough.
- Hold with the mechanism or a verified fact, never a preference: "Intentional, not an oversight: 9 workers = 2 x 4 CPUs + 1 for the 4-CPU host." "Verified against the installed 2.3.0 SDK; is_required() is True for all four fields".
- Defer with a reason and a trigger or a ticket: "Left as is for now; to be addressed before access control is turned on, tracked in the follow-up ticket."
